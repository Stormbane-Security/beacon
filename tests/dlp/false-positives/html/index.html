Phone Numbers and IDs

US phone format: 555-12-3456 is not a valid phone number.

Order number: 000-00-0000 (invalid SSN format)

Employee badge: 666-45-1234 (invalid SSN area code)

Ticket reference: 987-00-5432 (invalid SSN group)

Serial number: 123-45-0000 (invalid SSN serial)
